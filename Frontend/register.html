Member Access

First-Time Access

Member records are maintained by authorized Servant Leaders. Portal access is optional and must be created by the Member.

How member access works Member record → verified email → private portal password
  1. An authorized Servant Leader adds you to the Members database using your official email address.
  2. Use the same email address already stored in your Member record.
  3. Create your own private password and verify your email address.
  4. After verification, sign in to access the Member Portal.

Create / Claim Member Portal Account

Your email must already belong to a Member record. Contact an authorized Servant Leader if it does not.
