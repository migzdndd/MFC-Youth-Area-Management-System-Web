Member Access

First-Time Access

Regular member accounts are created automatically when an authorized Servant Leader adds a member to the official database.

How member access works Member record → temporary login → secure password
  1. An authorized Servant Leader adds you to the Members database using your official email address.
  2. The system creates your linked Member account and issues a temporary password.
  3. Sign in using that email address and temporary password.
  4. You will be required to create your own password before continuing to the portal assigned to your access level.

Regular Members do not self-register. This prevents duplicate or unlinked member accounts.

Not yet in the database? Contact an authorized Servant Leader.
