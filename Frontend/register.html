Accounts Are Created by your Chapter Servant

Accounts are linked directly to the official Members database and assigned by Area Servants

How first-time access works Area Servant adds member → account is created automatically
  1. Your Chapter Servant adds you to the Members database using your email address.
  2. The system generates a temporary password for your new account.
  3. Sign in using your registered email and temporary password.
  4. You will be required to create your own password before entering the portal assigned to your access level.

Self-registration is currently disabled to prevent duplicate or unlinked accounts.

Not yet in the database? Contact your Area Servant or Chapter Servant for more information.
