Member Access

First-Time Access

Regular Member records are created by authorized Servant Leaders/Admins from the Members dashboard. A Member record does not require a login account or password.

How member access works Member record → optional account access → Member Portal
  1. An authorized Servant Leader/Admin adds you to the Members database using your email address.
  2. Your Member record can exist without a login account.
  3. If Member Portal access is needed, an Admin uses the Members page login controls (Enable Login, Resend Setup, or Reset Login) to create or manage your login account.
  4. A secure password setup link can be sent to your registered email so you can choose your own password.
  5. A password is not required simply to be stored as a Member. It is only needed if optional Member Portal login access is enabled.

Regular Members do not self-register. The official Member record is created and managed by an authorized Servant Leader/Admin.

Not yet in the database? Contact an authorized Servant Leader.
